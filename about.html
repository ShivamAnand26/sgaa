PRACTICE OVERVIEW

Designing spaces that work for living.

Shilp Griha Associate is an architectural and interior design practice led by Ar. Vikas Verma. We offer practical, modern design for homes and commercial spaces, including 2D planning, 3D elevations, interior design and construction consultancy.

Our focus is on spaces that look good, work well and respect your budget, with Vastu-friendly planning where you want it. We work closely with every client, from the first idea to the finished drawings.
